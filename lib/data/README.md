# Distribution boundaries

`land.json` is generated by `node scripts/build-geography.mjs` from Natural Earth v5.1.2, 1:50m country boundaries. It records the source URL and SHA-256 of the downloaded source. Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/ .

Distribution version 2 samples continuous land coordinates, with equal bag counts per region (one cryptographically random extra side for odd totals). Country/polygon bounding boxes are weighted by spherical area, latitude is sampled uniformly in sine space, and points outside the land polygon or inside its holes are rejected. Rejection resamples the entire mixture. Country sizes, rural areas and northern latitudes are therefore treated consistently. Coordinates are persisted inside the same database transaction that registers the finalized launch and its bags.

USA includes the contiguous states, Alaska, Hawaii and mapped USA islands between 18°N and 72°N. Europe uses European country features within 32°W–60°E, 34°N–82°N, plus Cyprus and eastern Thrace. Overseas dependencies outside this envelope are excluded. The eastern edge of European Russia and the Turkish straits use approximate geographic cutoffs. These are generalized geographic boundaries, not parcel, access, hazard or navigability data; there is no city or public-park restriction. Coastlines and small islands have the resolution limits of the 1:50m source.

Historical version-1 curated locations remain in the database for transaction audit records. They are no longer seeded, shown as preview dots, or selected for new launches.
